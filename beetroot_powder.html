Understanding Urolithin A supplements and Mitophagy

Urolithin A supplements function by stimulating mitophagy, the selective recycling of damaged mitochondria within your cells. Mitochondria are the powerhouses responsible for generating ATP, the energy currency of your body. Over time, these structures accumulate damage, leading to reduced energy efficiency and increased cellular waste. By activating mitophagy, Urolithin A helps clear out these faulty components, allowing for the biogenesis of new, healthy mitochondria that operate at peak performance.
